Why blue-collar workers quit

Research by Mira · sourced 29 August 2026

Quit is not one behaviour. Employer “attrition” in the opened sources is mostly staffing-firm manufacturing temps. Construction, kilns, and circular return are different objects. Mixing them produces fake precision.

What reaches home

The month fails before payday. NiaSave is the member month: the shop, the hold, the send. This page is the record: what the opened sources say about why a contract workman leaves the floor, without pretending there is one national quit rate.

Blue-collar attrition is not one number

Each figure below is cited only as that source’s sample. None is Nia’s measured attrition.

  • TeamLease (Jan 2025) · manufacturing temporary workers · not Nia Over 43% leave within a year; 8.7% in 90 days. Method and n not on the opened press page.

    TeamLease, manufacturing workforce trends

  • Horizon PR citing BetterPlace + TeamLease · landlord PR · not Nia 8–24% monthly, 55% annual.
  • Lava HR in ETHRWorld (25 Jul 2025) · one employer GM-HR · not Nia 35–50% annual, entry-level factory, tier-1.
  • NITI SAFE (19 Dec 2024) · no percentage Links poor factory-adjacent housing to high attrition without a percentage.

    PIB, NITI SAFE press release

Wage delay

Aajeevika, 300 migrants: more than 68% had a major workplace dispute in the previous year, most often over wages. Delay held the worker hostage; leaving forfeited arrears.

Jayaram & Jain, Protection of Wages (Aajeevika)

City cost and housing

ILO 2020: Ahmedabad and Surat circular migrants spent 45–50% of income on food and fuel. NITI SAFE: housing as an attrition roadblock, no percentage attached.

ILO road map, 2020

Contractors and advances

Thekedar recruitment is the default. CPR brick-kiln fieldwork and the Working Group on Migration (NCR construction, n=150) describe advances that must be settled to leave.

CPR Roy & Kunduri, 2018 · Working Group on Migration

Festival and injury shocks

Harvest, monsoon, Holi, kiln close. A trip home without paid leave can cost the job. Injury is industry exit around 35–40 (Sharma et al. as cited in Aajeevika/ILO), not a 90-day spike. Do not use 2020 1.14 crore as the baseline.

What the data cannot say

There is no current national stock of blue-collar internal migrant wage workers. Census 2011 ~454 million is all migrants, mostly marriage — not Nia’s workforce count. Do not average 15m / 41.4m / 60m / 100m. Nia’s own attrition: unknown. Do not fill.
